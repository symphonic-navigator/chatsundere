## Staying safe in Chatsundere

### How Chatsundere protects your data

Your data is end-to-end encrypted. The server only ever stores encrypted
blobs — it never sees your messages, your keys, or your passphrase. Not the
operator, not the admin, nobody. That's what *zero-knowledge* means: we
**cannot** read your data, even if we wanted to.

### What that means for getting back in

Because we can't see your data, **we also can't recover it for you.** There's
no "reset" button an admin can press — the encryption that protects you from
everyone else protects you from us too.

Your **recovery key** is the one thing that can bring your account back if you
lose every device or forget your passphrase. Keep it, and you're safe. Lose it
*and* your devices, and the data is gone for good — by design.

### Keep your recovery key safe

We strongly recommend a **password manager**. Tools like **Bitwarden** or
**Proton Pass** have a secure notes field made for exactly this:

- It's encrypted, backed up, and synced across your devices
- You won't lose it if your phone breaks
- It's right there when you need it

Treat your recovery key like the master password to your whole digital life —
in Chatsundere, that's what it is.
