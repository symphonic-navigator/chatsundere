# Templates

A **template** (or *primer*) is a saved, simulated conversation you can drop into
a fresh chat **before** you send your first real message. It can carry an
optional opening greeting from the persona, plus a short back-and-forth that
sets the scene — so the persona picks up mid-thread instead of from nothing.

You **author and manage** templates here. You **apply** one from a *new, empty
chat*: open a chat that has no messages yet and choose **Seed from template**
near the composer. The greeting and turns appear as a primer block; your first
message continues from there.

Templates are plain text. A body alternates **you → persona → you → …**, always
starting with you. You can also create a template from a real conversation with
**Save as template** in a message's ⋯ menu.
