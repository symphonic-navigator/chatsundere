# Entrance Hall

The Entrance Hall is where every visit starts. The card at the top picks up
your latest chat or, on a first run, shows what is left to set up. Below it
are eight rooms, one line each:

- **My Circle** — your companions: start or continue a chat, or create a new companion.
- **My History** — every conversation, the most recently active first, plus your bookmarked messages.
- **My Treasury** — the artefacts your companions make and the replies you keep: documents, small web apps, code.
- **My Projects** — coming after the alpha; greyed out until then.
- **My Knowledge** — libraries of your own documents that companions can read from.
- **My Integrations** — outside tools your companions can call mid-conversation (MCP servers), such as searching a wiki.
- **My Settings** — AI providers and their keys, web access, voice, images and what companions know about you.
- **My Account** — your name, biometric unlock, recovery key, server linking and logout.
