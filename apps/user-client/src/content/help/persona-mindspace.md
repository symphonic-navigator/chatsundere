# Mindspace

Choose the visual atmosphere for this persona — the background colour, texture, and feel that surround your chats.

## Colour

Pick a mindspace colour scheme. Each colour maps to a curated palette that tints the background, surfaces, and accent elements. Selecting **Use user default** removes the per-persona override and falls back to whatever you have set in **My Settings**.

## Texture

Three overlays to layer on top of the colour:

- **Cloudy** — soft diffused gradients, calm and neutral.
- **Aurora** — sweeping ribbons of light, more dynamic.
- **Grain** — a subtle film grain, grounding and tactile.

Changes take effect immediately as a live preview inside the picker.
