# Logout

**Sign out** ends your session. Your encrypted data stays on this device, ready for next time.

**Delete all my local data** wipes everything on this device permanently — every companion, chat and setting. If your account is linked to a server, your encrypted data stays there and your recovery key can bring it back; on a local-only account there is no recovery. You'll be asked to type your username to confirm.

**Delete my account everywhere** (linked accounts only) deletes your account and all encrypted data on the server, then wipes this device. There is no recovery. If you are the primary admin, transfer the role to another admin in the admin console first.
