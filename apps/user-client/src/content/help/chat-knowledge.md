# Chat Knowledge

The knowledge libraries attached to **this chat** — the conversation you have open right now.

## What lives here

This page shows which of your knowledge libraries the companion can draw on during this
conversation. Libraries listed here are actively available to the companion as context when
it composes replies.

## Scope

The knowledge shown here is the intersection of what you have in **My Knowledge** and what
the persona for this chat is configured to use. Changing the selection here adjusts the
companion's access for this chat only; it does not alter your global library collection.

## Distinct from My Knowledge

**My Knowledge** (reached from the main menu) is your full personal library system — all
libraries, all documents, across every chat and every persona. This page is a chat-scoped
view: it answers the question "what can my companion see right now?" rather than
"what do I own?"
