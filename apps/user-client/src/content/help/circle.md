# My Circle

My Circle is your roster of AI companions. Each persona has its own
personality, instructions, and model — conversations with each one stay
separate and private on your device.

## Starting a conversation

Tap **Continue** on a row to pick up where you left off, or **New Chat**
to open a fresh session. If a persona's AI provider has not been
configured yet, the button is disabled and a "Provider missing" link
takes you straight to the provider settings.

## Creating a persona

Tap **+ New persona** at the top of the list to open the creation flow.
You can set the name, colour, font, instructions, and which AI model the
persona uses — everything can be adjusted later from the persona's hub.

## Per-persona actions

The **⋯** menu on each row holds everything else:

- **New chat** — start a fresh conversation with this persona.
- **New incognito chat** — coming soon; a session that leaves nothing in
  memory.
- **Continue** — resume the most recent conversation (disabled when none
  exists yet).
- **Go to persona** — open the persona's hub to edit its settings,
  knowledge, integrations, and more.
- **Delete…** — permanently remove the persona and all of its chats.
  This cannot be undone.

## Adult-mode filtering

When adult mode is off, personas marked as NSFW are hidden entirely. The
empty state looks identical whether you have no personas at all or they
are all filtered — nothing hints at what is out of view. Toggle adult
mode from the pill in the top bar.
