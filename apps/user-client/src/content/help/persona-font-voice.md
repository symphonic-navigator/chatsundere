# Font & Voice

Configure how this persona presents itself visually and audibly.

## Font

Choose the typeface used for this persona's messages in chat:

- **Sans** — clean, modern, formal. Good for assistant-style personas.
- **Serif** — warm, literary, informal. Suits storytelling or character personas.
- **Cursive** — expressive, dolce vita. For personas with a distinctive, playful flair.

The font is a property of the persona, not the mindspace — each persona in your circle can have a distinct typographic voice.

## Voice

Select the TTS voice used when reading this persona's messages aloud. Requires a voice provider (xAI or nano-gpt) configured in **My Settings → Voice**.

## Narrator Voice

Appears when Roleplay is enabled. Sets a separate voice for asterisk narration (e.g. *she reaches out and takes your hand*). Leave unset to fall back to the main voice.
