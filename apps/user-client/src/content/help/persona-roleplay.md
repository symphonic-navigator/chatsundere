# Roleplay

Configure whether this persona behaves as a roleplay character and how it greets you at the start of every new chat.

## Roleplay

When enabled, the persona stays fully in character, keeps replies short and conversational, and wraps narrative descriptions in asterisks. Turn it off for a regular assistant style.

## Narration

Controls the grammatical perspective of asterisk narration:

- **First person** — the persona narrates as itself (*I reach out and take your hand*).
- **Third person** — the persona narrates about itself from outside (*She reaches out and takes your hand*).

Narration has no effect when Roleplay is off.

## Greeting

When enabled, the persona opens every new chat with a freshly generated message composed from your rules below. The greeting fires once, before your first message.

Write the rules in the text field — describe the tone, setting, or opening line you want. Leave the field empty and the greeting cannot fire; the amber warning will remind you.

The Greeting toggle is only available when Roleplay is on.
