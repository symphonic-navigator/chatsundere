# Chat Bookmarks

Bookmarks and the table of contents for **this chat** — the conversation you have open right now.

## What lives here

Any message you have bookmarked in this conversation appears in this list. You can jump back
to it, remove the bookmark, or use it as a starting point for a new thread. Bookmarks are
scoped to this chat: they do not mix with bookmarks from other conversations.

## Table of contents

When a companion writes a long reply with headings, an automatic table of contents entry is
created alongside the bookmark. Use it to navigate multi-part answers without scrolling.

## Distinct from My Treasury

**My Treasury** (reached from the main menu) is your global collection across every chat and
every persona. This page shows only the bookmarks that belong to this specific chat. Think of
Treasury as the library and this page as the chapter you are reading right now.
